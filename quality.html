Quality Control

Our Quality Assurance Process

Quality is not just a checkpoint — it is woven into every stage of our manufacturing process.

Quality First

Zero Compromise on Quality

At Shyam Weavetech, quality is the cornerstone of everything we do. From the moment raw materials arrive at our facility to the final dispatch of finished yarn, every step is governed by strict quality protocols and consistent monitoring. Our commitment to quality ensures that every batch we deliver meets — and exceeds — the expectations of our valued business partners.

Our Process

6-Step Quality Control Process

A systematic, end-to-end quality framework that guarantees consistency and excellence in every cone of yarn we produce.

01
🔬

Raw Material Inspection

Every incoming batch of POY (Partially Oriented Yarn) is inspected for denier accuracy, tensile strength, and uniformity before being cleared for production.

02
📊

Production Monitoring

Continuous monitoring of machine parameters — twist per meter, tension, and speed — ensures consistent output throughout the production run.

03
🧪

In-Process Testing

Regular sampling and testing of yarn in-process to detect any deviations early and maintain batch-to-batch consistency.

04
✅

Quality Assurance

Finished yarn undergoes comprehensive quality assurance testing including evenness testing, strength measurement, and twist count verification.

05
📦

Packaging Standards

Yarn is wound on standardized cones, weighed precisely, labeled accurately, and packed securely to maintain quality during transit.

06
🔍

Final Inspection

A final visual and physical inspection of every lot before dispatch ensures only perfect product reaches our customers.

Our Standards

Quality Standards & Commitments

Our quality framework is built on industry best practices, consistent testing protocols, and a culture of continuous improvement.

⚖️

Denier Accuracy

Every batch is tested to ensure the yarn denier matches exact specifications — 75D or 50D — with minimal deviation tolerance.

🔩

Twist Consistency

Twist per meter (TPM) is monitored and verified throughout production to ensure uniform crepe texture in every cone.

💪

Tensile Strength

Yarn strength and elongation are rigorously tested to ensure performance reliability on high-speed weaving machines.

🎯

Zero Defect Dispatch

A comprehensive final inspection ensures that only defect-free, perfectly wound, and correctly labeled yarn leaves our facility.
