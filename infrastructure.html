Factory Overview

Built for Precision,
Designed for Scale

Shyam Weavetech's manufacturing unit is a purpose-built facility designed around one core goal — delivering premium crepe yarn with unmatched consistency. Established in Surat's thriving textile hub, our factory combines industrial-grade machinery with systematic workflows to produce yarn that meets the highest standards of the global textile industry.

From raw material intake to final packaging, every square foot of our facility is optimized for quality and efficiency. Our production floor maintains clean, organised conditions that directly contribute to product consistency and worker safety.

Technology & Equipment

Modern Equipment,
Consistent Results

At Shyam Weavetech, we operate a fleet of advanced industrial machines purpose-built for crepe yarn production. Each machine is calibrated to deliver precise twist levels, uniform denier, and consistent filament distribution — the key parameters that define premium quality crepe yarn.

Our false-twist texturizing machines and high-twist crepe twisting systems are maintained by skilled technicians and undergo regular servicing to ensure zero compromise on output quality. We continuously invest in process upgrades and machine calibration to stay ahead of industry demands.
