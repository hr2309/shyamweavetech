About Shyam Weavetech

From a single machine in 2019 to a trusted B2B crepe yarn manufacturer serving 100+ clients in Surat — our journey is built on quality, integrity, and dedication to excellence.

7+ Years of Excellence
Our Journey

From Vision to Manufacturing Excellence

Shyam Weavetech was founded in 2019 as Shree Vaishnodevi Texturised Yarn by Rameshbhai P. Sutariya and Harsh R. Sutariya with a vision to deliver premium crepe yarn to the Indian textile industry.

Over the years, we have grown from a single machine operation to a full-fledged manufacturing unit with 5 industrial machines, serving 100+ satisfied clients across Surat region.

In 2026, we rebranded to Shyam Weavetech — a name that reflects our growth, ambition, and commitment to weaving excellence into every product we make.
