Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions from our textile industry partners about our crepe yarn products and ordering process.

The numbers refer to denier and filament count. 75/72 means 75 denier with 72 individual filaments — a slightly heavier, fuller yarn ideal for structured fabrics like dress materials, sarees, and export garments. 50/48 is a finer yarn at 50 denier with 48 filaments, producing a softer, more delicate hand-feel suited to premium sarees, chiffon, and luxury garments. Both are high-twist crepe yarns, but the 50/48 delivers a finer, more fluid crêpe texture.
Crepe yarn is a textured polyester yarn that has been subjected to a high-twist process to give fabrics their characteristic crinkled, grainy surface texture (the "crêpe" effect). Our manufacturing process starts with premium POY (Partially Oriented Yarn), which is texturized using false-twist machines to add bulk and softness. The yarn is then subjected to high-speed twisting — either S-twist or Z-twist — at precisely controlled TPM (twists per metre) to achieve the crepe effect. The finished yarn is wound onto cones, inspected, and packed for dispatch.
